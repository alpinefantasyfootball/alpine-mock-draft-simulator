Two differences are known, applied rather than reported, and
must not be `fixed' by taking their column:
  * nflverse counts a touchdown as a first down; Sleeper does not.
  * Sleeper counts a blocked kick as a miss; nflverse does not.

Sleeper's first-down definition changed between 2018 and 2020. In 2018,
42 of 55 first-down lines match nflverse RAW (touchdown counted); from
2020 on, 91-99% match nflverse MINUS touchdowns, which is what this
audit applies. 2019 is the changeover and matches neither cleanly. So
pfd, rfd or cfd disagreeing on an old season is expected; on 2024 or
2025 it is not. (Measured 27 August 2026.)

Sleeper against itself: field goals by distance
Every made and missed kick should sit in exactly one distance band,
because app.js scores the made bands and now offers the missed ones.
This is where that is checked, in both directions.

Sleeper's own totals against Sleeper's own distance bands. The
totals are trustworthy either way -- fga == fgm + fgmiss for every
kicker season -- so a shortfall here is a band that was never
populated, not a kick that did not happen.

Bands are complete from 2024 on and lossy before it,
and the two halves are lossy to very different degrees in this pool.
The made bands very nearly reconcile throughout; the miss bands do not,
leaving a third to a half of every pre-2024 miss in no band at all. So a
league that scales a miss by distance gets a penalty that fires on about
half the misses on an old season, while fgmiss itself stays whole. That
is the reason the bands are an extra on fgmiss rather than a
replacement for it, and it is visible on the Seasons tab.

3 seasons where the bands EXCEED the total they
decompose. A part cannot be larger than its whole, so this is a
miscount at source rather than the sparse history above -- and it
nets against a shortfall in a season total, which is why it is
counted on its own:
  2019 | 2747 | fgm 23 | bands 24 | +1
  2018 | 3451 | fgm 37 | bands 38 | +1
  2018 | 3678 | fgm 28 | bands 29 | +1

Every season from 2024 on reconciles exactly, both directions.
