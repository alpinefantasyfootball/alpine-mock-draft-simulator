01Using Juke

Open the page and draft — that's the whole of it. There's no sign-up, no age gate and nothing to install. By using the site you're agreeing to these terms; if that's not something you're willing to do, the only thing to do differently is not use it, since there's no account to cancel or delete.

Don't try to abuse the service — scripting requests at it, attempting to bypass the room's rate limits, or trying to break into a room you weren't invited to. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what a room holds and for how long.

02Rooms and other people

A shared room is other people's picks and other people's chat messages, not just yours. Juke mechanically strips anything that isn't plain text or a real GIPHY link out of chat, and rate-limits how fast any one person can act — but it does not moderate what people say to each other beyond that. You're responsible for what you type, and for who you send an invite link to.

03Where the data comes from

Player data, ADP and statistics come from Sleeper and Fantasy Football Calculator's public feeds; player news headlines come from Tank01. Juke links to that news rather than republishing it, and doesn't claim to have written any of it. Every rating, grade and suggestion on the site is Juke's own calculation on top of that public data, not an endorsement from any of those providers, and not expert analysis from anybody on staff — there isn't one. See how it works for exactly how each number is computed.

04No guarantees about the football

Every grade, projection and suggestion in Juke is a model's best guess at a game that hasn't been played yet, offered for a mock draft — practice, not the real thing. None of it is a promise about how any player will actually perform, and none of it is advice for money on the line, in a real draft or anywhere else.

05The service as it is

Juke is provided as it stands, with no warranty that it will be available, uninterrupted or error-free. It depends on other people's servers — Sleeper, Fantasy Football Calculator, Tank01, ESPN, Cloudflare — and an outage on their end can mean a feature here goes quiet. The app is built to keep drafting even when that happens, but that's an engineering goal, not a guarantee.

Juke is free, and to the extent the law allows it, is offered without liability for damages arising from your use of it. Nothing here is meant to override rights you have that can't be signed away where you live.
